Story

About Bianca

Born in Divinópolis, Bianca started playing futsal at eight and moved to eleven-a-side football at thirteen, when she passed the trials at Clube Exemplo.

A defensive midfielder who reads the game well, she stands out for ball recovery and the forward pass that speeds up the transition to attack. She is vocal on the pitch and captained the U-17 side.

In 2025 she was a starter in the run to the state U-20 final. She is now looking for her first opportunity with a team in the second or third tier of the Brazilian Women's Championship.
